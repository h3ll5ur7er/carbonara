## Decisions

- **Where the game's numbers live.** Module effects, the beacon and the quality chain are a dozen constants in [[packs/factorio/plugin/effects.ts]], with their Lua source, as the Satisfactory plugin does with its Docs values. The pack contract has no place for module or beacon prototypes, and per-recipe facts only fit in tags: the importer writes `ignored-by-productivity:<item>=<amount>` and `no-quality` tags, `energy.drain` on buildings, and fixes `modules.effects` for prototypes without `allowed_effects` (every effect). No contract changes.
- **Modules per node:** a count per kind plus one tier for the machine's modules and one for the beacons'. Mixed tiers in one machine aren't expressible (YAGNI). Modules beyond the slots are left out in field order, with a warning; productivity modules on a recipe without productivity are left out, with a warning. A module fits where every effect it improves is allowed (beacons take speed modules despite their -quality, not quality or productivity modules), which matches the prototypes and the wiki.
- **Beacons:** n beacons pass on n × 1.5 × profile[n]; profile[n] = 1/√n cut to four decimals, which reproduces the prototype's 100-entry `profile` exactly. Beacon power needs a "machines per beacon" field, since beacons are shared; beacons built = machines built × beacons in range ÷ machines per beacon.
- **Power:** working machines × usage × (1 + consumption) + machines built (rounded up) × drain. Burners report `fuel` (MW) instead of `power`.
- **Quality scope:** item variants (`id@uncommon` … `id@legendary`) for every solid item, generated by the importer from the dump's quality prototypes (1,436 items instead of 304); a node's `quality` field (the level it crafts at) and the quality chance as expected values, 2.1 rules (`next_probability` 1, `chain_probability` 0.1). Recyclers need nothing extra: recycling recipes are ordinary recipes, so upcycling loops are cycles the LP solves. Deferred: quality of machines, modules and beacons (their bonuses).
- **Heat and fluid temperatures: deferred.** The pack has no power generation at all (boilers, steam engines and turbines, reactors, heat exchangers, fusion are dropped by the importer), and steam has no recipe besides acid neutralisation. Recipes only set the temperature of what they make (acid neutralisation's 500 °C steam; hot and cold fluoroketone are separate fluids anyway) and none limits an ingredient's temperature, so temperatures change no rates in the pack today; they matter once engines and turbines are in. Heat needs a custom `reactor` node kind (compiler work, [[Architecture/Plugins]]). Both belong with a power-generation task.
- **Space Age:** one save setting, on by default. Off, every expansion mod's recipes (`space-age`, `quality`, `recycler`, `elevated-rails`) are unavailable (a warning, still solved), as are expansion buildings and quality (warnings from `validate`). The data stays the Space Age dump's, so base recipes Space Age changed keep their Space Age numbers.
- **Not modelled:** productivity research (so the +300 % `maximum_productivity` cap is unreachable and not applied; no 2.1.14 recipe changes it), pollution, the game's 0.01 % effect resolution (differences in the fifth decimal), surface conditions, spoilage.
